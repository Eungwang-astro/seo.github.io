Research

I search for lensed gravitational-wave signals and build pipelines that turn lensing observables into cosmological inference.

Lensing of gravitational waves

Finding signals that a galaxy or cluster has magnified, delayed or duplicated.

Standard-siren cosmology

Measuring H0 and characterising dark matter with gravitational-wave sources.

Microlensing and substructure

Probing small-scale lenses and dark-matter substructure.

Source and lens populations

Modelling which sources and lenses we expect to observe and their population properties.
